Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Motabhai Electronics mobile application (“App”) collects, uses, and protects information. The App is intended only for authorized employees and partners of Motabhai Electronics.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username/login details used to access the App.
  • Business data: sales orders, deliveries, outstanding bills, purchase and stock-related information entered or viewed in the App.
  • Location data: precise location while the App is in use, only when needed for delivery tracking and delivery confirmation.
  • Device permissions: camera/photo library access when used for delivery confirmation or related features.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide App functionality such as login, orders, delivery confirmation, and reports.
  • To capture delivery location accurately while confirming deliveries.
  • To support Motabhai Electronics internal business operations.

4. Location

Location is requested only while using the App (When In Use). It is used to capture delivery location and confirm deliveries. We do not use location for advertising.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed for Motabhai Electronics business operations and may be stored on company systems/servers used by the App.

6. Data Retention

Business and account-related data are retained as needed for Motabhai Electronics operational and record-keeping requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed through the App. Users must keep login credentials confidential.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children and is intended for authorized business users only.
